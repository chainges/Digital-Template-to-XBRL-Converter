Climate change may affect the business through both physical and transition risks. Physical risks include disruptions caused by extreme weather events, rising temperatures, flooding, or other climate-related hazards that could impact operations, supply chains, infrastructure, and employee safety. Transition risks may arise from evolving environmental regulations, carbon pricing mechanisms, changing customer preferences, technological advancements, and increased stakeholder expectations for sustainability. These factors could lead to higher operating costs, compliance requirements, and the need for investments in low-carbon technologies and more resilient business practices. The company continues to monitor climate-related risks and integrate appropriate mitigation and adaptation measures into its business planning to strengthen long-term resilience.
